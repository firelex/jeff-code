## Method

- Pass rate interval: Wilson 95%. Paired intervals: percentile bootstrap, 4000 resamples of whole tasks (seed 20261005), so the attempts and rounds of a task move together. McNemar p: exact binomial test on the blocks only one side passed. An interval marked 'n/a' had resamples where the statistic is undefined (no block in the set, or no pass).
